# Image prompt kit
Formula: **[subject] [action/pose], [setting], [style/medium], [lighting], [camera/lens/composition], [palette], [mood], [aspect]** + negatives.
Styles: editorial photo · 3D clay render · flat vector · isometric · watercolor · line art · cinematic still · product studio shot
Lighting: soft window light · golden hour · rim light · studio softbox · neon · overcast diffuse
Composition: rule of thirds · centered symmetric · close-up 85mm · wide 24mm · top-down flat lay · negative space left for text
Negatives: blurry, extra fingers, watermark, distorted text, low-res, oversaturated
Product: "on seamless #F4F4F4 backdrop, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, 100mm macro"
Thumbnail: big face/emotion or object, 3 words max, high contrast, 16:9
Iterate: change ≤2 variables per round; lock seed when the tool supports it.
